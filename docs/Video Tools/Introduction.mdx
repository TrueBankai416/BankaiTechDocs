# Video Tools Overview

This section covers open-source, command-line tools for downloading, converting, and managing video content from YouTube and other platforms.

## Tools Covered

### yt-dlp

**Best for: Downloading videos from YouTube and 1000+ other sites**

[yt-dlp](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp) is a free, open-source command-line tool that lets you download video and audio from YouTube, Vimeo, Twitter/X, SoundCloud, Instagram, Reddit, TikTok, and hundreds of other websites. It is the actively maintained community fork of the original `youtube-dl` project.

**Pros:**

- ✅ Open source and actively maintained
- ✅ Supports 1000+ sites
- ✅ Flexible output format and naming options
- ✅ Can extract audio-only (MP3, AAC, FLAC, Opus, etc.)
- ✅ Playlist and channel downloads
- ✅ Subtitle, thumbnail, and metadata embedding
- ✅ Faster updates than the original youtube-dl
